Lymph drains the fluid that leaks from capillaries back to the blood. Lymph nodes (lime) filter it along collecting vessels (pale green) that join into trunks: the jugular, subclavian and bronchomediastinal trunks on each side, and the lumbar and intestinal trunks below. The right lymphatic duct drains the right head, neck, arm and chest into the right venous angle. The thoracic duct, which starts at the cisterna chyli, drains everything else into the left venous angle.
