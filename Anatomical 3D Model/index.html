Before you begin

This atlas shows the whole body, including the external genitalia and the breast. Choose whether to show these regions.

Treated as private parts:

  • The external genitalia (penis and scrotum, or vulva) and the buttocks.
  • The female breast, including nipple and areola. The male chest is not treated as private.

Hidden regions are covered by a neutral swimwear shape. The internal organs, such as the uterus, ovaries and prostate, stay available.

You can change this later under Body.

Anatomical directions

  • Superior / Cranial toward the head.Inferior / Caudal toward the feet.
  • Anterior / Ventral toward the front.Posterior / Dorsal toward the back.
  • Medial toward the midline.Lateral away from the midline.
  • Proximal nearer the limb's attachment.Distal farther from it.
  • Superficial toward the surface.Deep away from the surface.
  • Afferent toward a centre, such as sensory nerves to the CNS.Efferent away from it, such as motor nerves.
