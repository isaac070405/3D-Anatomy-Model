## Chinese labels

Each structure name, group heading, system and layer shows its Traditional Chinese name after the English one, in the sidebar lists, the tree, search results, the hover label, the selection tab and the pin. The button under the introduction turns them off, and the choice is remembered in the browser. Typing Chinese in the search box (for example 股骨) finds the matching structures.

`zh-hant.js` holds one `English name|中文名` line per term, 1,100+ terms. Left and right, numbers, vertebra and nerve codes, tooth positions and "Base, qualifier" names are built from those terms, which covers all 2,348 structure labels. To fix or add a name, edit its line.
